Candidate Helpdesk FAQ

Common Questions & Answers

Quick answers to what candidates ask most frequently before registering.

Exam slots are allocated immediately upon completing registration and assessment fee confirmation. Slots run every Saturday and Sunday with flexible morning and afternoon windows. You can also reschedule your slot up to 24 hours prior without additional charge.

Our proctoring system autosaves candidate progress continuously per question. If a disconnect occurs, you can reconnect within 15 minutes to resume where you left off. In rare catastrophic outages, our support desk can grant an emergency re-attempt token.

Every InternBoot Level Certificate includes an immutable Certificate Number and QR Code. Anyone or any hiring partner can visit Certificate Verification to validate candidate authenticity, awarded level tier, and exam score timestamp.

Candidates achieving Level 3, 4, or 5 are automatically ranked and presented to our network of 650+ hiring partners. Top level achievers receive priority interview callbacks and expedited screening within 5 business days of certificate generation.

InternBoot standardizes fresher talent assessment and connects ambitious graduates with verified internships and full-time hiring partners across India.
